A little about us

Built Around a Teacher's Day.

tution app was shaped around the everyday challenges tuition teachers face. Between teaching lessons, recording attendance, checking fees and answering parents' messages, the work outside the classroom can take up too much time.

We wanted one straightforward place to organise these daily tasks. That is why tution app brings fee tracking, attendance, test reports, parent updates and a business dashboard together in one simple experience.

The aim is practical: help teachers spend less time on repetitive administration and more time helping students learn.

  • Simple, approachable tools
  • Less repetitive daily work
  • Convenient parent communication
  • Organised student records
  • Designed around tuition needs
